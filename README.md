# 📱 Android Work at Veel

I'm an Android Developer at [**Veel**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.veel.app&hl=en&pli=1), a creator app and my office product.

[![Get it on Google Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get%20it%20on-Google%20Play-3DDC84?style=for-the-badge&logo=googleplay&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.veel.app&hl=en&pli=1)

**What I've built and shipped:**

- 💳 **Payments**: Khalti and HamroPay for Nepali users, Mercury and Stripe for international users
- 👛 **Wallet features** for creator earnings and payouts
- 🔴 **Go Live campaigns**, with live streaming integrated using Amazon IVS
- 🤝 **Partnership campaigns** connecting creators and brands
- 📹 **Video upload refactor**: reworked the major video upload flow for phase one, replacing the earlier background video upload
- 💸 **Affiliate programs** and campaign integrations across collab screens
- 📈 **Social media analytics dashboard** for creators
- 🛠️ Many smaller features, critical bug fixes and enhancements across the app

**How I build:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Apollo GraphQL, Hilt, and Clean Architecture (use cases, repositories, mappers, MVI-style ViewModels).
